Configurable APH Approval Route Matrix

Applications dynamically follow different stage sequences as per AICTE Approval Process Handbook

1. Route: Only Scrutiny
Applicable to: Standard Extension of Approval (EoA) with zero deficiency
Stages: Registration → Scrutiny → Final Approval
2. Route: Scrutiny + EVC
Applicable to: New Technical Institution (LoA), New Course, or Increase in Intake
Stages: Registration → Scrutiny → Re-scrutiny (if flagged) → EVC Inspection → Final Approval
3. Route: EVC + SAC/SHC
Applicable to: Appellate cases, Restoration of Intake, or Conditional Compliance Hearing
Stages: Registration → Scrutiny → EVC Inspection → SAC/SHC Hearing → Final Approval
4. Route: Only EVC
Applicable to: Change of Site / Location or Direct Infrastructure Verification
Stages: Registration → EVC Inspection → Final Approval
